[SKILL] freelance-niche
[OBJ] Help a freelancer choose a profitable, defensible niche and the ideal client profile (ICP), then build a plan to become a recognized authority in it.

[RULES]
1. [REQ] Niche selection matrix: score candidates on (a) demand, (b) willingness to pay, (c) your skill fit, (d) competition density, (e) margin. Pick the highest composite, not the trendiest.
2. [REQ] ICP definition: firmographics (size/industry/location) + psychographics (pain/budget/decision) + channel (where they hang out). One ICP per niche.
3. [REQ] Authority ladder: publish 1 pillar per quarter in the niche, collect 3 case studies, speak/guest where the ICP is. See `personal-branding` + `content-marketing`.
4. [CMD] Context7 IDs: `plausible/analytics` (demand signals), `google/ga4` (search demand).
5. [REQ] Validation step: before committing, run 5 client interviews or analyze 20 job posts in the niche to confirm pain + budget. No niche chosen on vibes.
6. [REQ] Pricing alignment: niche sets pricing ceiling — pair with `pricing-strategy`. Specialized niches command 2-3x generalist rates.
7. [REQ] Arabic/RTL: if targeting Arabic market, evaluate local niches (e.g., Gulf fintech, MENA e-commerce) with SAR/AED budgets; tone via `arabic-freelance`.
8. [REQ] Differentiation: write a one-line positioning vs generalists and vs adjacent specialists. Store in `brand-kit`.
9. [REQ] Exit criteria: niche is "validated" when you have a named ICP, 3 proof points, and a repeatable lead source.
10. [REQ] Re-evaluation: revisit quarterly; pivot only with demand data, not boredom.

[PROHIBIT]
1. No niche without documented demand proof.
2. No ICP that is "everyone."
3. No pivot without data.
4. No Arabic niche without local currency/budget check.
