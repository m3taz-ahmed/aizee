[SKILL] content-marketing
[OBJ] Build a compounding content asset engine: define pillars, produce evergreen + timely pieces, repurpose across formats, and distribute through every channel.

[RULES]
1. [REQ] Pillar model: 3-5 core topics (pillars) → cluster articles/videos → internal links back to pillar. One pillar per quarter minimum depth.
2. [REQ] Editorial calendar: 4-week rolling plan with format (long/post/video/thread), channel, owner, status. Cross-link `social-media-marketing` for scheduling and `email-marketing` for nurture.
3. [CMD] Context7 IDs: `gitroomhq/postiz-app` (distribution), `knadh/listmonk` (newsletter), `plausible/analytics` (performance).
4. [REQ] Repurpose rule: 1 pillar → 1 long-form → 4 social posts → 1 email → 2 short videos → 1 infographic. Maximize output per input.
5. [REQ] Quality bar: every piece has a hook, a promise, evidence (data/case), and a CTA. Use `copy-frameworks` (AIDA/PAS) for the CTA block.
6. [REQ] SEO alignment: cluster content follows `seo-lord` brief (keywords, outline, E-E-A-T, internal links). Never orphan a cluster piece.
7. [REQ] Free-first tools: Postiz for distribution, Listmonk (AGPL) for newsletter, Plausible for measurement. Notion/Sheets as calendar fallback.
8. [REQ] Arabic/RTL: produce ar + en versions for each pillar when audience is bilingual; mirror calendar; RTL typesetting checked before publish (link `arabic-freelance`).
9. [REQ] Performance loop: monthly review of top/bottom 3 pieces by engagement+conversion; double down on winners, retire losers.
10. [REQ] Evergreen refresh: update pillar content quarterly; set "content decay" alert (≥25% traffic drop) per `seo-lord` opportunities.

[PROHIBIT]
1. No content published without a channel + CTA assigned.
2. No plagiarism or undisclosed AI ghostwriting where disclosure required.
3. No Arabic asset shipped with broken RTL.
4. No calendar that ignores measurement feedback.
